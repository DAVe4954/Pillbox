## 4. Your data and privacy

- Pillbox keeps everything on your own PC: settings, clipboard history, the shelf, reminders,
  notes, play time and logs. The author receives nothing.
- **Clipboard history** stores what you copy, on your PC. Unpinned copies are deleted after two
  days, or after 1, 7 or 30 if you choose so. Pinned items and snippets stay until you remove them.
- Pillbox uses the internet **only for features you switch on**, and sends only what each one
  needs:
  - **Weather, air quality and place search:** your location's coordinates, or the place name
    you type, go to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com). Weather data by Open-Meteo.com,
    under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Open-Meteo's free service
    is for non-commercial use.
  - **Currency rates:** the currencies you chose go to [Frankfurter](https://frankfurter.app).
  - **Lyrics** (off by default): the song's title, artist, album and length go to
    [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net).
  - **Website check and ping:** the addresses you add are contacted directly.
  - **Claude usage numbers** (off by default): Pillbox reads your own Claude sign-in file on your
    PC and asks Anthropic for your usage, as the Claude apps themselves do.
- These services have their own terms and privacy policies. The author does not control them
  and is not responsible for them.
- **Phone transfer** opens a small server on your home network only while you use it, protected
  by a random one-time token. Windows may ask you to allow it through the firewall.
